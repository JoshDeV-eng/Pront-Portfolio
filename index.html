About

I'm a developer who spends more time thinking about how an interface feels than how it looks in a static comp. My work sits at the intersection of motion, geometry, and everyday usability — real-time 3D scenes, physics-driven interactions, and interfaces that respond to how you actually move a cursor.

Outside of client work, I build small WebGL experiments, contribute to open-source tooling, and write about performance budgets for the web.
